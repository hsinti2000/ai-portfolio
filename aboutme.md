## Featured Projects

### 1. AI-Assisted Gait Training System
**Thesis Project**  
**The NDT Gait Training System with Auditory and Power Assistance System**

Developed an intelligent rehabilitation gait training system integrating **robust control theory**, **AI gait detection**, **auditory feedback**, and **power assistance modules**.  
The system was designed to provide real-time cues and support, helping users maintain a more stable and rhythmic walking pattern during rehabilitation training.

**Highlights**
- Integrated AI gait event detection with assistive system design
- Applied control methods to improve system stability and performance
- Combined sensing, feedback, and power assistance into a practical rehabilitation system

---

### 2. Clinical Trials of a Stroke Rehabilitation Trainer Employing a Variable-Speed Treadmill
**Clinical Collaboration Project**  
Collaboration with **Cheng Hsin General Hospital**

Participated in a clinical research project on a stroke rehabilitation trainer using a variable-speed treadmill.  
This project focused on the practical validation of rehabilitation system performance and clinical usability in real-world medical settings.

**Publication**
- *Sensors* 2025, 25, 5834

---

### 3. Gait and Balance Assessment in Elderly Individuals with Knee Osteoarthritis Using IMU Technology
**Clinical Collaboration Project**  
Collaboration with **Cheng Hsin General Hospital**

Worked on gait and balance analysis for elderly individuals with knee osteoarthritis using IMU-based sensing technology.  
This project involved motion-related data interpretation, system application, and clinical research support.

**Publication**
